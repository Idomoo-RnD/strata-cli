# Kinetic type / captions — route

**This is the route when** the brief says kinetic, typographic, type-led, text-heavy, captions, subtitles, lyric, quote, headline. The test: the words *are* the picture; remove the type and nothing is left.

**It expects** the copy, final or near-final, and the font files; often a VO or a track to time against. **It delivers** a type-led piece: the *Kinetic typography / title pack* row in [blueprints.md](../blueprints.md); **kinetic-typography**, **type-in-space** or **swiss-grid** in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- Kinetic type or captions, per text layer.
- The hero word per beat, and what the rest of the line does.
- What the type is timed to: speech (`strata captions`), music (`strata beats`), or its own read time.
- One entrance, one exit, one emphasis for the piece.

## Where it usually sits on the range
Read time sets the floor, so it sits between the rows: holds long enough to read twice, transits as fast as a word can land. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).
