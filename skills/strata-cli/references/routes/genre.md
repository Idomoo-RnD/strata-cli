# Genre feel — route

**This is the route when** the brief says thriller, comedy, documentary, noir, horror, drama, epic, heist, western, sci-fi. Against [feel-like.md](feel-like.md): a genre is a *grammar*; a named film or campaign is a *signature*. Genre is a modifier on another kind, never the kind itself.

**It expects** a kind already chosen (an ad, an explainer, a sting) plus the tonal word. **It delivers** that kind with one genre's grammar applied: its row in the *Genre control matrix* of [editorial/narrative-genres.md](../editorial/narrative-genres.md), and the nearest built look in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- The dominant mode, with this genre as a modifier, and how many modifiers in total.
- What the audience tracks, and how information is released.
- Which of the genre's default picture and sound biases the piece takes, and which it refuses.
- The rhythm shape; played straight, or as a wink.

## Where it usually sits on the range
The matrix's *rhythm shape* column is the answer: thriller tightens toward the *film, action* row after long holds; drama and documentary sit near *film, quiet*; comedy is tension and release. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Read, in this order
- [editorial/narrative-genres.md](../editorial/narrative-genres.md): the *Genre control matrix*, then the genre's section; *Hybrid selection examples*.
- [reference-styles.md](../reference-styles.md): rows carrying a genre as a signature (Hitchcock suspense, neon noir, documentary vérité).
- [motion/08-styles-index.md](../motion/08-styles-index.md): *5. Industry and genre conventions*; execute the look in video-styles.md.

## Watch for
- a genre applied as a transition preset or a grade: [editing-director.md](../editing-director.md), *Non-negotiables*
- every influence averaged into a generic fast montage: [editing-director.md](../editing-director.md), *Reference map*
- the genre's own traps: [editorial/narrative-genres.md](../editorial/narrative-genres.md), *Failure modes* under the chosen genre

## Done when
- the storyboard names the dominant mode, the genre modifier and what the audience tracks; `strata review`'s Story row passes
- the rhythm shape shows in `shots.lengths`: intervals tighten, or waves escalate, as declared
- the sound bias is audible where the genre section puts it
