# Supplied material — route

**This is the route when** the user hands over a storyboard, script, shot list, PDF, deck, reference sheets, footage or a voice sample ("here is", "attached"). Against [footage-edit.md](footage-edit.md): material *describing* a piece to be made is intake; footage that *is* the piece is an edit.

**It expects** every attachment opened before any plan. **It delivers** what the material specifies, at its own duration, format and shot order; the shot table in [intake.md](../intake.md) is the storyboard.

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- What each input *changes* (brief, brand, cast, copy, timing, look, format), and which change nothing.
- Frame-driven or reference-driven, scene by scene.
- Who speaks on camera, and only them; what is voice-over.
- Which person, product or set recurs, and its continuity ranking.

## Where it usually sits on the range
Wherever the material puts it: a storyboard carries its own timecodes; a script or deck wants read time and leans toward the *film, quiet* row. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Read, in this order
- [intake.md](../intake.md): effect map, shot table, mode per scene.
- [production-bible.md](../production-bible.md): sheets and identity blocks for recurring principals.
- [video-generation.md](../video-generation.md): prompting each routed scene; clip length against its slot.
- [video-generation-advanced.md](../video-generation-advanced.md): the talking-scene recipe; `--ref-video` to copy supplied footage's edit.
- [workflow.md](../workflow.md): check A0, a supplied storyboard is executed, not redesigned.

## Watch for
- an attachment planned from its filename, not opened: [intake.md](../intake.md), *0. Open everything, and say what it changes*
- a speaking clip plus a narration layer with the same line: [traps.md](../traps.md), *The traps, in full*
- supplied sheets regenerated instead of used: [production-bible.md](../production-bible.md), *2. Make one sheet per principal*

## Done when
- every shot-table row has a clip at its in/out point; `strata review` reports no freeze in any slot
- `strata captions` on each talking clip returns that row's line
- the storyboard presented back is the material's shot table plus an execution plan
