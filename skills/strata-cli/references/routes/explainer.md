# Explainer / testimonial / internal / recap — route

**This is the route when** the brief says explainer, tutorial, how-to, testimonial, case study, internal comms, onboarding, recap, highlights, demo, feature tour. Against [tv-ad.md](tv-ad.md): the viewer must *understand* or *believe* something afterwards; a single claim and a CTA is the ad route.

**It expects** real information that must stay truthful. **It delivers** a longer landscape piece built for comprehension: the *Explainer (faceless)*, *UI walkthrough / product demo* or *Website / app showcase* row in [blueprints.md](../blueprints.md); **motion-explainer**, **testimonial**, **screen-capture** or **event-recap** in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- The instructional objective (recognise, understand, perform, decide, adopt, trust), which the edit must make observable.
- Narration-led or picture-led: whether a radio edit comes first.
- Who speaks: a presenter on camera ([presenter.md](presenter.md)), a voice-over, or neither.
- What counts as evidence, and what is illustrative and must be labelled.

## Where it usually sits on the range
Toward the *film, quiet* row: fewer, longer shots because the viewer is processing, with a compression or two where a montage earns its place. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Watch for
- narration, captions, graphics and B-roll competing at once: [editorial/nonfiction-formats.md](../editorial/nonfiction-formats.md), *Failure modes* under *Corporate, internal communication, education, and explainer*
- B-roll that only repeats the spoken words: [editing-director.md](../editing-director.md), *Non-negotiables*
- a testimonial sentence the participant did not make: [editorial/nonfiction-formats.md](../editorial/nonfiction-formats.md), *Testimonial-specific integrity*

## Done when
- every step or claim has a shot whose dwell passes `strata review`'s Typography row

- the recap shows setup and consequence, not only the impact; the muted watch still teaches
