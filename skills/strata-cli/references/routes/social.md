# Social / vertical / UGC / trailer — route

**This is the route when** the brief says social, TikTok, reels, shorts, vertical, 9:16, UGC, trailer, teaser, hook. Against [tv-ad.md](tv-ad.md): the viewer can scroll past it, probably muted; a fixed placement they sit through is the ad route.

**It expects** a message and a platform; sometimes a long master to recut. **It delivers** a short vertical piece: the *Social promo* row in [blueprints.md](../blueprints.md); **ugc-selfie**, **paid-social** or **footage-repurpose** in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md); for a trailer, *Trailer and teaser* in [editorial/short-form-performance.md](../editorial/short-form-performance.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- The hook (result-first, problem-first, character-first), and that it connects honestly to the payoff.
- Muted-first or sound-on: what the piece delivers with no audio.
- Native texture or polished; if native, what stays controlled beneath it.
- Loop back to the opening, or end on an action.

## Where it usually sits on the range
Toward the *showreel, 10 s* row: short shots, a wide spread between a flash and the proof hold; that hold separates it from noise. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Done when
- `strata review`, muted watch: claim and brand read from picture and text alone
- `strata preview --grid` at every key frame: no text or face inside the platform's UI zones
- the proof beat is the longest shot and its frames move; `shots.lengths[0]` is not a logo
