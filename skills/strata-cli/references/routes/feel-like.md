# "Make it feel like X" — route

**This is the route when** the brief says feel like, looks like, in the style of, inspired by, a reference film or campaign, "like Apple / Nike / Kubrick". Against [genre.md](genre.md): a named work or maker is a *signature* to measure; a genre word is a *grammar*. A brand named as the client is neither.

**It expects** a reference (a name, or better a clip) and the kind it is applied to. **It delivers** that kind carrying the reference's measured signature: its row in [reference-styles.md](../reference-styles.md) and that row's *Nearest built style* in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- What is borrowed (moves, timing, discipline) and what is not (their palette on this brand).
- A clip to `strata deconstruct`, or the five signature answers written down.
- Which of the reference's numbers the piece adopts and which the subject bends.
- Whether the reference's *edit* is wanted too (a clip → `--ref-video`).

## Where it usually sits on the range
Where the reference measures: `strata deconstruct` on the clip gives shot lengths, energy, stillness and loudness directly. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Read, in this order
- [reference-styles.md](../reference-styles.md): the named row; the closing paragraph for a name not in the table.
- [motion/08-styles-index.md](../motion/08-styles-index.md): the broad index for an era or design school; name it here, execute in video-styles.md.

## Watch for
- the reference's surface copied instead of its technique: [reference-styles.md](../reference-styles.md), the opening paragraph
- a client's name treated as a style request: `strata route` matches a name only behind a style preposition
- a brand's atoms replaced by the reference's: [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md), *Read these two rules before using anything here*

## Done when
- `strata review --reference` shows the render's tokens beside the clip's; the gaps are the ones the storyboard chose
- the signature's moves are visible while every colour and typeface is the brand's
- the storyboard names the row and what in this brief bends it
