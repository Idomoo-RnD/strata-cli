# Brand — route

**This is the route when** the brief says brand, branded, on-brand, style guide, brand book, "like the last one", a series, our colours, our fonts. Against [feel-like.md](feel-like.md): the brand owns the atoms and wins; a reference lends technique.

**It expects** brand material (logo, brand book, style guide, existing videos, a website, a Figma file, fonts) or an existing `.brand/brand.md`. **It delivers** the kind's piece authored from the brand document, offered and signed off before the storyboard when none exists.

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- Does `.brand/brand.md` exist (read and follow it); if not, build one now, or author this one piece without.
- Which inputs the atoms come from, and every gap named rather than invented.
- Font files on disk, and whether they carry the copy's scripts.
- What the brand fixes beyond atoms: motion language, pacing, layout.

## Where it usually sits on the range
Where the brand's motion language puts it, if the document defines one; otherwise where the kind puts it. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).
