# Music-led / beat-synced — route

**This is the route when** the brief says music, beat, BPM, track, song, audio-reactive, visualiser, rhythm, soundtrack. Against every other kind: the track is decided *before* the picture and the picture answers it.

**It expects** a track, or a prompt to generate one, and the mix target for where it plays. **It delivers** a piece cut and animated on measured onsets: **audio-reactive** in [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- The track first: supplied, or generated with a stated BPM and a timeline arc.
- Which onsets carry cuts (the bar), which carry accents, and what reacts to the envelope.
- The loudest and quietest beat, at timecodes, and the gap between them.
- A drop, stop or silence, and what the picture does there.

## Where it usually sits on the range
The track's map is the range: verse holds, chorus flashes. Cut rate leans toward the *showreel, 10 s* row while energy inside shots follows the arc. The brief decides; declare the four numbers from a measurement (review.md §7).

## Done when
- `strata review`: the cut list sits on `strata beats` onsets within the tool's tolerance
- integrated LUFS at the declared target; the two named beats measure the declared gap
- the drop or silence shows in the energy profile by thirds; the track does not die before the end card
