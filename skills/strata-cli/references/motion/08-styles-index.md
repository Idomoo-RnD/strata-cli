# Style and era index (broad)

> ⛔ **A brand document overrides every style named here.** If `.brand/brand.md` exists it wins -
> palette, typeface, type ramp, radii, shadows, motion language, pacing - always, silently, without
> asking. A style contributes **structure and technique**; the brand owns the **atoms**.
> See [brand.md](../brand.md).
>
> **This file is the BREADTH index: one line per style, the look plus its signature motion cue.**
> For the styles we have actually built and rendered, [video-styles.md](../video-styles.md) carries
> the deep entry - shot grammar, cut rhythm and the measured tells. **Route here first to name the
> look, then use that file to execute it**; only fall back to the one-liner below when it has no
> deep entry. Neighbouring entries here are the place to borrow cues from.

## 1. Graphic and design-system styles
- **Flat design**: no depth, solid colors; slide plus fade, no shadows, quick.
- **Flat 2.0 (corporate flat)**: flat with subtle shadows and gradients; explainer staple; smooth ease-in-out.
- **Material**: surfaces with elevation; shared-axis and container transforms; emphasized easing.
- **Apple fluid**: glossy, precise, slow product orbits, spring UI, light sweeps.
- **Swiss (International)**: grid, neutral sans, asymmetric type, restraint; text slides on grid lines, hard cuts.
- **Bauhaus**: primary shapes and colors; rotations and constructive assembly of circles, squares, triangles.
- **Minimalism**: few elements, generous space; one slow move per scene, long holds.
- **Maximalism**: layered, loud, dense; constant motion, collage, pattern scrolls.
- **Memphis**: 80s postmodern squiggles and confetti shapes; bouncy, playful rotations.
- **Art Deco**: gold, symmetry, fans and rays; radial reveals, elegant wipes, metallic sweeps.
- **Bento grid**: tiled modules of varied sizes; cascade reveals, tile flips, module resizing.
- **Neo-brutalism**: thick outlines, raw colors, hard shadows; snappy cut-ins, no soft easing.
- **Editorial (magazine)**: big serif headlines, photo crops, captions; line-mask text, slow pans on photos.
- **Scandinavian and Japanese minimal**: natural palettes, quiet motion, breath-like pacing.
- **Corporate memphis (flat people)**: oversized-limb characters, flat scenes; gentle bobs and slides.

## 2. Retro and era styles
- **Y2K**: chrome, bubbles, gradients, pixel UI; glossy flips, bubble pops, sparkle particles.
- **Vaporwave**: pastel grids, statues, VHS; slow-scrolling grids, glitch, sunset gradients.
- **Synthwave (outrun)**: neon grids, sun stripes, chrome text; forward camera over a grid, glows.
- **80s broadcast**: wipes, star fields, chrome extrusions; radial wipes, flying logos.
- **90s web (early digital)**: pixel fonts, marquees, GIF stepping; 8-12 fps.
- **Mid-century modern**: flat illustration, mustard and teal, textures; hand-drawn boil, paper cut-outs.
- **70s psychedelic**: wavy type, hue cycles, kaleidoscopes; liquid distortion.
- **Retro-futurism**: optimistic space-age shapes; orbits and rocket arcs.
- **Film noir (analog cinema)**: grain, vignette, slow dissolves; light shafts.
- **Super 8 and home video**: gate weave, light leaks, jump cuts; warm flicker.

## 3. Illustration and character styles
- **Character (illustrative)**: rigged characters, scene builds; overlapping action and secondary motion.
- **Cel (toon shading)**: 3D with flat lighting; stepped on twos.
- **Anime-inspired**: speed lines, dramatic holds, smears; impact frames.
- **Paper cut-out**: layered paper with shadows; hinge rotations, pop-ups.
- **Stop-motion (claymation look)**: stepped frames, slight jitter, tactile materials.
- **Collage (mixed media)**: cut photos, textures, scribbles; quick cut-ins, rotation jitter.
- **Risograph (print)**: grainy, misregistered layers; layers slide into registration.
- **Comic (halftone)**: panels, bursts, onomatopoeia; panel wipes, pop-ins.
- **Whiteboard (hand-drawn)**: lines drawn by an implied hand; path trim everywhere.
- **Line art (mono-line)**: thin consistent strokes; draw-on and morph.
- **Pixel art**: quantized motion, palette cycling, 8-12 fps.
- **Doodle (scribble)**: boil, loose paths, annotation over footage.
- **Isometric illustration**: parallel-projection scenes; builds along axes, conveyor flows.
- **Children's book**: soft textures, gentle bounces, page-turn transitions.

## 4. Digital, tech, effects-led styles
- **Glassmorphism**: frosted blur cards; slow parallax, refraction shimmer.
- **Neumorphism**: soft extruded UI; subtle press states.
- **Claymorphism**: puffy 3D; squash-friendly bounces.
- **Futuristic HUD (FUI)**: rings, scanlines, readouts; rotation, decode text, thin strokes drawing.
- **Cyberpunk**: neon on dark, glitch, rain; chromatic aberration, flicker.
- **Glitch (datamosh)**: error as aesthetic; block displacement, frame drops.
- **Acid graphics**: distorted type, chrome, 3D blobs; liquid morphs and warps.
- **Grainy gradients**: soft color fields with noise; drifting mesh gradients.
- **Neon signage**: tube strokes with glow and flicker.
- **Holographic**: iridescent shifts tied to rotation.
- **Chrome (liquid metal)**: reflective 3D type; turning light sweeps.
- **Dark-mode tech**: deep backgrounds, glowing accents; additive light motion.
- **Generative (abstract)**: noise fields, particles, procedural patterns; continuous evolution.
- **Data-art**: data drives form; particles and charts as art.
- **ASCII (terminal)**: monospace, typewriter, cursor blink; stepped.
- **3D satisfying (soft-body)**: physically simulated loops; slow motion and squish.
- **SaaS gradient (Stripe-like)**: gradients, glass objects, precise UI mockups; slow orbits, smooth fades.
- **Blueprint and wireframe**: construction lines, dimension callouts; lines draw in, labels tick on.

## 5. Industry and genre conventions
- **Sports broadcast**: slashes, speed, bold condensed type; whip transitions, aggressive wipes.
- **News broadcast**: lower thirds, tickers, boxes; fast, symmetric, reliable.
- **Fintech (banking)**: clean, trustworthy, precise counters; restrained easing, no overshoot.
- **Luxury (fashion)**: slow, editorial, serif; line masks, tracking reveals.
- **Automotive**: light sweeps, low cameras, reflections; orbit and push-in.
- **Food and beverage**: splashes, pours, organic motion; slow-mo liquid, warm grade.
- **Beauty**: soft light, gloss, macro; slow drifts, shimmer.
- **Real estate**: smooth pans, floor-plan builds; isometric reveals.
- **Kids and education**: bouncy, bright, big; squash and stretch, sound pops.
- **Medical (pharma)**: clean, calm, precise; soft ease-in-out, diagrams drawing on.
- **Gaming**: juice, impact, particles; overshoot and screen shake.
- **Music video**: cuts on beat, flashes, audio-reactive elements.
- **Documentary**: restrained, informative, typographic; slow fades, archival treatment.
- **Explainer (illustrated world)**: flat illustrated environments, smooth isometric builds, friendly characters.
- **Title sequence**: mood-driven, slow, conceptual; typography as protagonist.
- **Social (short-form)**: vertical, hook-first, captions, fast cuts, loops.
- **E-commerce (product demo)**: the product stays hero; UI callouts, zoom to detail.
- **Personalized video**: data hero moments, name and number reveals, clean frames for variables, templates that survive any value length.
- **Events and conference**: countdowns, speaker lower thirds, big stage-safe type; loops for idle screens.
- **Travel and hospitality**: slow pans over imagery, warm grade, map routes drawing.
