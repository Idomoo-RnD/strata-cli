# Clone a reference video — route

**This is the route when** the brief hands over a video and asks for it again with something changed: clone, remake, recreate, "like this video but with our product", "this but in Spanish", "do this for us". Against [feel-like.md](feel-like.md): a *style* is borrowed and applied to a new piece; a *clone* keeps the piece and replaces what is in it. Against [supplied-material.md](supplied-material.md): a storyboard is executed; a reference video is first read into one.

**It expects** the reference opened, measured and written down as three notes before any asset exists. **It delivers** the reference's structure carrying the user's cast, product, copy and brand — and none of the original's assets or identity ([clone-a-video.md](../make/clone-a-video.md)).

## Settle these before the storyboard (into BRIEF.md; unattended → decide and record in decisions.md)
- What is kept (beats, rhythm, layout, where things land) and what is swapped (cast, product, copy, brand, music).
- The three features whose loss would make it stop resembling the reference.
- Whether the reference clip may be uploaded for `--ref-video` — consent, non-sensitive — or the camera comes from the animatic.
- Who speaks, and whether a re-voice or translation is planned (events re-anchor on the new take).

## Where it usually sits on the range
Where the reference sits: `strata deconstruct` gives its shot-length range, energy and rhythm, and the clone adopts them unless the Treatment says which ones the new subject bends. Label anything not measured as inferred.

## Read, in this order
- [clone-a-video.md](../make/clone-a-video.md): the procedure — read, write down, keep/swap, rebuild, re-anchor, prove.
- [intake.md](../make/intake.md): the shot table the Timeline extends; *Audit the brief for conflicts*.
- [production-bible.md](../make/production-bible.md): sheets for the new cast, product and set.
- [presenter.md](presenter.md): when a host speaks on camera.
- [ref-video.md](../shoot/ref-video.md) and [sketch.md](../shoot/sketch.md): the two ways to carry the camera and the cut.
- [captions.md](../shoot/captions.md): phrase-level timing for the reference and for the new take.

## Watch for
- the reference's footage, logo, voice or a face reused: [clone-a-video.md](../make/clone-a-video.md), *What a clone is, and is not*
- events timed by hand from the old take after a re-voice: [clone-a-video.md](../make/clone-a-video.md), *Re-anchor on the new performance*
- a request that is really a style: [feel-like.md](feel-like.md)
- the reference clip uploaded without consent: [upload.md](../shoot/upload.md)

## Done when
- `strata review --reference reference.mp4` shows the render's tokens beside the reference's; the gaps are the Treatment's choices
- the three load-bearing features are present at their beats, and every text and media role that must stay dynamic is a named placeholder
- nothing of the original's assets or identity is in the frame
