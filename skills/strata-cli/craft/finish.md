# Rhythm, polish and the craft check

Part of [craft.md](../craft.md). The [design contract](../make/design-contract.md) governs intent,
and [review](../qa/review.md) governs acceptance. These are craft defaults, not a requirement to
add activity or effects to every frame.

### Rhythm & continuity

- Use measured audio onsets when syncing to music, but choose whether the picture leads, lands or
  deliberately lags; not every cut must hit every beat. [generative-fx.md](generative-fx.md).
- Establish the actual destination mix requirements. A silent piece or a deliberate quiet interval
  is valid. Voice, bed and SFX are available roles, not three mandatory audio layers. Prepare fades,
  ducking and trim-ins externally where the engine cannot keyframe them; [music.md](../shoot/music.md).
- Shape the sequence around information and emotion. Contrast can strengthen an impact; uniform
  rhythm can be purposeful. Judge the arc, not a minimum range or energy ratio.

### Polish & anti-patterns

Motion should express material, attention and timing. Linear travel is correct for constant speed;
a steep settle suits a rigid arrival; overshoot belongs where physical/brand character earns it.
Avoid repeating the same unchosen entrance everywhere, not consistency itself.

Source coverage must be honest: never disguise exhausted footage as a deliberate locked hold.
An intentional loop or still frame can be valid when designed and approved. Flat composition
can be premium without added camera depth or lighting. Check [anti-slop.md](anti-slop.md) for
reflexive decoration and [motion-design.md](motion-design.md) for technique options.

### Craft check (before I call it done)

- Does the approved viewer promise land, and does every beat earn its place?
- Are focal order and typography clear at actual viewing size and through movement?
- Do the shortest/longest shots carry their intended jobs? Are locked/live holds as planned?
- Do actual copy, data, marks, material and sound match the approved direction?
- Are diagnostics interpreted with source/confidence rather than optimized blindly?
- Has the final candidate passed the regression gate, with remaining limitations disclosed?

A poster answers layout questions only. Inspect the MP4, strips and sound evidence; never invent
a viewing or treat a clean compile as a creative pass.
